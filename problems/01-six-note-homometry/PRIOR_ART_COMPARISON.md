# Prior art and novelty limits

**The exact criterion is verified; its novelty remains unresolved.** This is
a scoped summary of the manuscript's research record, not a new exhaustive
literature review. An absence from the inspected statements is not a proof of
originality.

## Established ideas

- One-pitch shifts and their musical interpretation are established in
  Althuis and Göbel, *Z-related pairs in microtonal systems* (2000), Section 6:
  [primary report](https://ris.utwente.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/5115044/1524.pdf).
  The inspected families concern four and five notes.
- The regular-polygon-plus-two-point construction is attributed to Patterson,
  *Ambiguities in the X-Ray Analysis of Crystal Structures* (1944), p. 199,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.65.195), by Goyette's dissertation,
  pp. 75–76:
  [full dissertation](https://urresearch.rochester.edu/fileDownloadForInstitutionalItem.action?itemFileId=76476&itemId=24908).
  Relevant Goyette sections were inspected; the original Patterson page was
  not. This construction is prior art and is not claimed as new.
- Masai and McShane, *Isospectral configurations in Euclidean and hyperbolic
  geometry*, Section 8, provide invariant-component constructions using
  involutive isometries:
  [author manuscript](https://macbuse.github.io/MC0726.pdf).
- Collins, *Transposition Invariance and Parsimonious Relation of Z Sets*
  (2001, revised 2002), gives a one-step obstruction, an earlier special case
  of a generating-displacement restriction:
  [author-hosted report](https://composerprogrammer.com/research/selftransposingz.pdf).
  Indexed primary text was inspected, but the full report was not retrieved.

## Narrow candidate contribution

The statement under consideration is the exact all-modulus **existence**
criterion for strict six-note pairs sharing five notes, including necessity:
`n >= 12` and (`3 | n` or `4 | n`). The inspected construction statements do
not themselves establish this complete iff. That limited comparison is not
an originality certificate.

Erickson and Jones's unrestricted classification question for six or more
points, [Problem 5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.08997v4#S5), remains broader
than this result. Their open problem does not establish novelty of the
one-exchange criterion.

## Remaining review

Direct inspection of Patterson p. 199, Soderberg's original dual-inversion
paper, and Collins's complete report remains necessary before a publication-
level novelty assertion. Further searches could still locate the exact
criterion or an equivalent statement. The manuscript separately proposes an
order-four quotient count, but that count is not Lean-formalized and its
novelty is likewise unconfirmed.
